Models and training

Dataset

Flickr8k: 8,000 images with 5 human captions each, split by image into train, validation, and test sets (about 80/10/10). Splitting by image keeps captions of one picture out of both training and test data.

Architecture

A frozen ResNet-50 turns each image into 2048 numbers. A linear layer maps them to the starting memory of a 2-layer GRU, which writes the caption one word at a time. Only the GRU side is trained.
