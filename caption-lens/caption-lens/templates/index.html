Upload any image and two models describe it: BLIP, a pre-trained vision-language model, and our own CNN + GRU trained from scratch. Everything runs on your machine.

Caption an image How it works

The problem

Computers see pixels, people read sentences. The system must recognize what is in a picture and say it in grammatical English, for example "A dog is running in a grassy park."

Our approach

We use a pre-trained BLIP model as a working captioner, then train our own CNN + GRU captioner and measure how close it gets. No paid APIs are used.

What this page does

It is the front end for the project: upload an image, see both models' captions side by side, and review the evaluation results.

Dataset
Flickr8k, 8,000 images
Encoder
ResNet-50, frozen
Decoder
2-layer GRU, 512 hidden
Baseline
BLIP base
